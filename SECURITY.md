# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@qafila.io** with the details. **Do not open a public issue** for security problems,
and do not disclose the issue publicly until we have assessed and addressed it.

Please include, where possible: what the problem is, how to reproduce it, the impact you see, and any
proof-of-concept. Do not include third-party personal data.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement within 3 working days** that we received your report.
- **An initial assessment within 10 working days**, with our understanding of the impact and the
  likely next steps and timeline.

We will keep you informed as we work on a fix and will credit you if you would like that, once the
issue is resolved.

## Scope

This covers all Qafila Robotics repositories and services: the fleet platform (control plane, AI
gateway, fleet memory, orchestrator, dashboard), the edge agent and robot adapters, the website, and
the organisation's infrastructure.
